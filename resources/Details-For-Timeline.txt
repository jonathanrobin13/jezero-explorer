Before the Hackathon

You're welcome to explore the challenges, gather relevant datasets and resources, learn new tools, and connect with teammates ahead of time. However, project development must not begin before Saturday, November 14, at 9:00 AM local time.

During the hackathon, we'll keep our Philadelphia virtual meeting room available and host a few informal check-ins. You're welcome to join whenever you have questions or want to connect with others, but there's no need to attend every check-in. Feel free to focus on your project!

If you need assistance finding resources or navigating the event, please don't hesitate to reach out.

Most importantly, remember to submit your project before the official deadline: Sunday, November 15, at 11:59 PM local time. Only projects submitted on time can be considered for judging and awards.

3. Project Submission Guidelines & Tips

Please review the attached Project Submission Guidelines & Tips for information on preparing and submitting your project.

A few important reminders:

    Each team should designate a Team Owner responsible for submitting the project.

    Submissions should include a project summary, a demonstration (a seven-slide presentation or a 30-second video), a publicly accessible project link, and relevant data and resource references.

    Be sure to disclose any SI/AI tools used in developing your project.

    Don't wait until the last minute to submit! Allow extra time for uploading materials and resolving technical issues.

    Even if your project isn't fully completed, you're encouraged to submit what you've accomplished.
